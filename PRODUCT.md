# IncomeFlow

IncomeFlow is a testnet personal income organizer for independent professionals who receive discrete ERC-20 payments. Configure destination wallets and basis-point shares once; Reactive Omni observes deposits and requests authenticated vault allocations without a frontend or backend signer staying online.

Primary action: configure an owner-controlled vault and receive supported payments. Secondary action: inspect the genuine demo receipt and reproduce its origin/Reactive/callback evidence. Not a trading terminal, tax calculator, accounting replacement or audited mainnet product.

Explicit user brief supplies audience, core workflow, light premium financial direction, trust/precision/calm tone, testnet-only scope and evidence requirements. No additional interview is needed under the user's instruction to make supported decisions autonomously. Assumption: users want a readable desktop workspace that works equally well on a phone; not validated with customers.

Operating states: disconnected, wallet missing, wrong network, no vault, rules unset, transaction awaiting wallet/pending/confirmed/reverted, RPC unavailable, automation paused, funding needed, event observed, callback requested, distribution confirmed and recovery required. Unknown chain stages remain unknown.

Current source of truth: verified deployed contracts and docs/ONCHAIN_PROOF.md. M1 is proven on live testnets. Public sample calculator values are illustrations, never reported as live balances.
